Our Story

Charity water is commited to using 100% of the donations towards waterworks projects designed to bring fresh, clean, drinking water to communities without. From communities in Nepal, Madagascar and Uganda, your contribution helps further the dream of universal drinking water across the globe. Charity: water ensures that waterworks projects are also headed by leaders within the community in order to ensure that everyone's topmost priority is clean water for everyone.

Real Impact: Meet Amina

Amina used to walk miles every day to collect water for her family. Thanks to your support, her village now has a clean water well. Amina can go to school and her family is healthier and happier.
